The right fit

A wedding with a point of view.

The right celebration here feels intimate, not industrial. Here is how to tell if the estate is for you, and where it is not.

This may be your wedding if

You want intimacy over spectacle.

  • You would rather celebrate with 80 to 200 people you actually know.
  • You care how the place feels, not just how the stage photographs.
  • You like real flowers, candlelight, gardens and natural materials.
  • You want family and close friends to stay together.
  • You want one team to hold food, decor, media and the flow of the day.
Probably not the right fit if

You are picturing a big-production wedding.

  • You need a 500 to 1,000 guest banquet format.
  • You want a high-decibel DJ, fireworks or a nightclub-style after-party.
  • You want plastic-heavy decor or a stage that hides the property.
  • You want to bring many unrelated vendors and manage them yourself.
  • You are choosing only by the lowest per-plate price.

The filter is the point. The right couple reads this and thinks, this is us.

Karnataka heritage

A Wedding Rooted in Tradition

For families who wish, traditional Gau Pooja and Ashwa Pooja can become part of the wedding morning with the cattle and horse who live on our farm. A quiet Karnataka heritage wedding ritual, surrounded by nature, family and blessings.

01

Gau Pooja

With the cattle of the farm

The cows are washed, garlanded and fed by hand, and the family offers prayers before the wedding rites begin. An old Karnataka way of asking the household’s animals for their blessing on the marriage.

02

Ashwa Pooja

With the horse of the farm

The horse is honoured the same way, often before the groom’s procession. Kept quiet and unhurried — a blessing among family, not a performance for a crowd.

These rituals are offered only if your family wants them. The animals are residents of the working farm, and their comfort sets the pace — no crowding, no noise, no firecrackers.

Food is part of the memory

Not a banquet package. A wedding table.

Curated with the family and cooked fresh. Plated and generous, never a conveyor-belt buffet.

Breakfast

Puri-chole, paratha, poha or upma, jalebi, fruit, chai and coffee.

Moving starters

Paneer tikka, dahi kebab, seasonal bites and hot service through the gathering.

Chaat and high tea

Pani puri, bhel, masala puri, momos, tea, coffee and savouries.

Main meal

North Indian favourites, breads, dal, rice, seasonal vegetables, sweets and family requests.

Zero-proof drinks

Hibiscus, lime-mint, jaljeera, aam panna and seasonal coolers.

The menu is curated with the family, and seasonal availability guides the final choices. Orchid Tree is proudly BYOB.
